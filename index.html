        How to Play

        1. Split into two teams and enter your team names.
        2. Each turn, one player describes the target word without saying it or any of the 5 taboo words.
        3. Teammates shout out guesses before time runs out.
        4. Tap ✅ Correct when your team guesses right, ⏭ Skip to pass a hard card, or ✋ Taboo if a forbidden word slips out.
        5. Pass the device between turns. The team with the most correct guesses when the game ends wins!
